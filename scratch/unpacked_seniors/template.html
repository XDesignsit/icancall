How it works

You set it up. They just answer.

Three quiet steps, all on your side. Your parent never has to do a thing differently.

1

Claim their number

Pick one easy-to-remember iCanCall number for Mom or Dad. Write it on the fridge, program it as a speed-dial, or save it as "Family." That's the only number they'll ever need.

2

Add the family circle

Put yourself, your siblings, a trusted neighbor, and the care team in order — up to six people. You manage it all from your phone; nothing is installed on theirs.

3

They dial like always

When they call that one number, iCanCall cascades through the circle until someone answers — and everyone gets a text that the call came in. No menus to navigate unless you want them.
