How it works

Set it once. Trust it forever.

Three simple steps stand between you and complete peace of mind.

1

Claim your number

Pick a single, easy-to-remember iCanCall number. Share it once — on a bracelet, a fridge, a school form — and never juggle a list of numbers again.

2

Add your circle

Designate up to six trusted contacts and set the order they should be reached. Family, neighbors, caregivers, doctors — your circle, your priority.

3

Choose how to connect

Pick your routing in the dashboard: let iCanCall cascade through the circle until someone answers, or greet callers with a simple menu to reach the right person directly.
